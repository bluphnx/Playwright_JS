----------------------------------------------------------------
GRATUITY FORMULA
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Gratuity = (Last Basic Salary × 15 × Years of Service) / 26

  Where:
    Last Basic Salary = Monthly basic component (not CTC, not gross)
    15                = 15 days salary per year of service (Gratuity Act)
    26                = Working days in a month
    Years of Service  = Rounded to nearest full year
                        (if > 6 months → round UP)


----------------------------------------------------------------

  Service Period:   November 8, 2021 → August 28, 2026

  Rounded:          5 years (since 10 months > 6 months → rounds UP)

----------------------------------------------------------------
TAX IMPLICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - Gratuity is TAX-FREE up to ₹20,00,000 (as per current rules).
  - Since ₹84,857 is well below the ₹20L limit, you receive
    the full amount without any tax deduction.


----------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - Gratuity is calculated on BASIC pay only, not total CTC.
  - The rounding rule: if service exceeds 6 months beyond a
    complete year, it rounds to the next full year.
  - Gratuity is paid at the time of separation (resignation,
    retirement, or termination).
  - You must have completed 4 years 190 days (firm's policy)
    to be eligible. Standard Gratuity Act requires 5 years,
    but some firms have internal policies with shorter periods.
